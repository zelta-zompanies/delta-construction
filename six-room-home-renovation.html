Six Rooms, One Home: Before and After

The bathroom changed quite a bit, but the kitchen and hallway deserve a closer look, too. Navy cabinets, new flooring, and lighter paint gave this home a different feel from room to room.

A Closer Look

Walk Through the Changes

A few photos can introduce a project. These six comparisons give you a fuller look, from the tub and kitchen through the living spaces and hallway.

Each slider uses the original before and after photographs. Drag left or right to compare, or focus a slider and use the arrow keys. Home shows the after photo; End shows the before photo.

01Bathroom Tub

↔
BeforeAfter

The tub surround makes the bathroom transformation easy to see. Small white square tiles give way to larger gray-and-white patterned rectangles, with a recessed niche set into the back wall. The finished surround brings a different scale and a cleaner-lined appearance to the same view.

02Kitchen

↔
BeforeAfter

The kitchen moves from white cabinetry and dark counters to navy cabinets paired with light counters. Below them, the square tile floor gives way to wood-look flooring that continues the visual connection into the adjoining room. Lighter paint lets the navy stand out.

03Dining

↔
BeforeAfter

In the dining room, dark lower wall paneling and trim made a strong contrast with the blue-gray upper walls. The finished view brings those surfaces into a lighter palette, with white chair rail and trim defining the room. Wood-look flooring carries the change across the full space.

04Vaulted Living Room

↔
BeforeAfter

The vaulted ceiling remains a defining feature of this living room. Dark wood wall surfaces in the before view give way to lighter walls, while wood-look flooring changes the appearance below. The visible brick fireplace keeps a familiar focal point within the refreshed space.

05Front Room

↔
BeforeAfter

The front room follows the same direction: lighter walls, white doors, and wood-look flooring in place of the square tile. White trim frames the openings and connects the finish from one room to the next. Together, these visible changes give the room a more consistent appearance.

06Hallway

↔
BeforeAfter

The hallway brings the changes together between rooms. Square floor tiles and a brown door in the before photo give way to wood-look flooring, a white door, and lighter paint. The finished view carries the same palette along the length of the hall.
