Privacy & measurement choices

Updated October 24, 2026

Your inquiries

When you contact Delta Construction through our website or an advertising lead form, we use the contact information and project details you supply to respond to your inquiry and discuss your project. Our website forms are processed through Netlify. If you submit a form hosted by an advertising platform such as Google, that platform also handles your information under its own privacy policy.

Please include only the information needed to discuss your project. You can reach us at zach@901deltaconstruction.com or 901-568-4128 about your inquiry or privacy choices.

Basic Google measurement

For visits our hosting provider verifies as coming from the United States, basic Google Analytics and Google Ads measurement is on by default. This helps us understand website use and which advertisements bring inquiries. Measurement may use cookies and browser identifiers, page visits, interaction events, accepted form-submission events and call measurement. Google receives technical information such as your IP address and browser information when these requests are sent.

We do not send the names, email addresses, telephone numbers, messages or other field values from our website forms to Google measurement. Enhanced conversions, customer-data uploads, Google signals and personalized-ad use are disabled in our website tags. These tags are not used to collect retargeting audiences.

When call measurement is available, Google may provide a forwarding telephone number. The visible number and call link change together and still connect you with Delta Construction. If measurement is off or a forwarding number is unavailable, our real number remains available.

Google tracking stays off outside the United States, when the region cannot be verified, when you opt out, or when your browser sends Global Privacy Control. No Google measurement tag loads before the US eligibility decision. See Google's privacy policy (opens in a new tab) for Google's handling of information.

Your choice

Google measurement is off until this browser can verify eligibility.

Your choice is saved in this browser using local storage and a first-party preference cookie. It applies across this website and future visits in this browser. You can change it here or through the privacy link in any page footer. A Global Privacy Control signal always takes priority. Clearing browser storage or using another browser may require setting your choice again.

Turning off measurement does not prevent you from viewing projects, calling us or submitting an inquiry. The site still uses hosting and form services needed to deliver those functions. Already-sent measurement cannot be recalled by turning it off.

Other services and links

The service-area map uses map services, and the Keep Up page includes a Facebook feed. Those services may receive browser and connection information when used or loaded. This Google measurement choice does not control Facebook, map services or external websites you choose to open.
