About Us

Our Name Is Our Creed.

Creed Construction is a locally owned construction and renovation company serving homeowners and businesses throughout the Memphis area. Our focus is simple: deliver high-quality work, communicate clearly, and treat every project like it's our own property.

A creed is a set of beliefs you live by, and ours is simple: building code is the minimum, never the goal. Every job we take on is built to code and held to a higher one — the standard we'd want in our own homes.

Most of our work is residential: kitchens, bathrooms, finished spaces and full renovations. We work closely with each client from the first walkthrough to the last punch-list item.

We also handle design in-house. Layouts, finishes and materials get settled before work starts, so the finished space fits together.

Too many renovations come with vague pricing, slow answers and timelines that slip. We run ours on clear estimates, careful planning and steady project management, so you always know where the job stands and what comes next.

We'd rather earn your next project than make the most on this one. That means reliable work, straight answers, and nothing we wouldn't put our name on.

Our Process

How We Work

1

Initial Consultation & Estimate

We start with a conversation about your goals, timeline and budget. Then we visit the site, scope the work, and give you a detailed written estimate before anything begins.

2

Design & Project Planning

Once you approve the estimate, we lock in the design, materials and schedule, and we handle the permits.

3

Construction & Project Management

Our crew does the work. You hear from us as it moves: progress updates, photos, and any decisions that need you.

4

Final Walk-Through & Handover

We walk the finished job with you and fix anything that isn't right before we call it done.
